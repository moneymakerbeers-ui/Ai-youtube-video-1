So how long does it last? Mass decides. A heavier star burns its fuel much faster, just to hold up its own weight.
The Sun gets about 10 billion years. Some massive stars, only a few million.
Mass also decides the ending. Stars like the Sun fade quietly into a white dwarf, a cooling cinder the size of Earth.
Stars with more than about 8 times the Sun's mass end by exploding. And to leave a black hole, a star probably needs around 20 suns or more.
