# Style bible — "Deep Field Specimen"
**Topic read.** Subject: the life cycle of a massive star. Evidence type: real telescope observations (Webb, Hubble, Chandra, EHT, SDO) + physical models. Register: wonder, one short build of tension (core collapse), warmth (elements in us), calm close. Audience: general, all ages, phone-first.
**Presets.** 2 Deep Field (ground, light, grain) + 7 Specimen Studio (labels as scientific annotations, leader-free direct labels). Changed: real photographs are the hero layer (not code backdrops); labels carry a provenance tag on every image ("Real image · Webb" / "Illustration").
**Palette (computed, tools/contrast.py: all text pairs ≥ 8.4:1, graphics ≥ 4.1:1).** bg #07090f near-black (never pure black) · bg2 #121826 · fg #f3efe6 warm white · muted #aab3c5 · accent #ffb45e starlight amber (the thing being explained) · accent2 #86b9ff cool blue (gravity, geometry) · warn #ff6b5b · line #64728f. Looks: nursery (warmer accent), core (gold/ember), void (violet). Element colours in diagrams are always named in text (never colour alone, H7).
**Lighting logic.** One warm key = the star itself; everything else is lit by it or is cool ambient. No lens flares, no fake god-rays.
**Texture/grade.** Fine static film grain 0.018, vignette 0.32, contrast 1.05, saturation 1.04, 3 % amber tint over every source so Webb, Hubble, Chandra and generated plates sit in one world.
**Typography.** Inter (SIL OFL, files in library/fonts/Inter). Labels 700, numbers 800, context 600; min 44 px at 1080 short side. Captions: karaoke, amber highlight, 6 words max, translucent ground.
**Motion language.** slow-drift: every plate breathes with a slow eased push or pull (soft easing) that has a stated reason; diagrams build on their words; cuts by default; dissolves for time passing; 2 signature moments (nested zoom clump→protostar; core-collapse reveal of the supernova). No whooshes, no shake, no flash: the supernova brightens by easing, never a white frame.
**Composition.** full-bleed plates for real images; centred specimen for diagrams; recomposed tall frames for Shorts (subject in the central 60 %, labels above the caption band).
**Must not look like.** generic neon sci-fi; lens-flare space opera; a slideshow of unlabelled pretty pictures; AI images passed off as photographs; text baked into images.
**Dimensions.** palette family: cool-night · texture: film-grain · motion language: slow-drift · composition: full-bleed-plate.
