STAGE 04 - MOTION. Follow CLAUDE.md §4, §7, `reference/transition-grammar.md` and `reference/qa-gates.md`. Video folder: `videos/<slug>/`. No paid calls in this stage.
RESUME: read `state.json`; keep passing work.

THE ENGINE ALREADY EXISTS. `engine/` is shared: `render.ts` draws the scene graph in Chromium (Playwright) from `render/plan-<target>.json`, frame by frame with a fixed frame-to-page assignment (reproducible output), and pipes it to ffmpeg. `make_plan.py` turns `scenes.json` + the alignment into that plan (timing truth, transition windows with their anchors, captions). Do not rebuild it; extend it only when a video needs something it cannot do, and then add a test (`tests/engine/*.test.ts` for math, `tests/*.py` for behaviour). Entities are images, SVG, frame sequences, text, code visuals and (loudly) placeholders for assets that are missing.

1. VISUALS (code). Built-ins: `flow`, `aspect_frame`, `bars`, `counter`, `wave`, `arrow`, `grid`. A graphic that is specific to this video goes in `videos/<slug>/visuals/index.ts` (default export `VisualDef[]`: `id`, `aspect(props)`, `create(host, props, init)` returning `{ update(ctx) }`, `motion: "continuous"` when the picture keeps changing after it has entered, optional `path()` so a truthful morph can deform it). A project visual overrides a built-in of the same id. Draw with the style tokens, never hard-coded colours. Check the registry: `npx tsx engine/visuals-manifest.ts --project videos/<slug> --out videos/<slug>/render/visuals-manifest.json`; the plan uses it to know which visuals legitimately move, so a deliberate rest is told apart from a stall.
2. LAYOUT PASS. Finalise landscape placements for all scenes; finalise portrait layouts for every Short scene by recomposing, not cropping. `npx tsx schema/validate.ts project videos/<slug>` checks boxes against the portrait margins, minimum text size, overlaps and entities outside the frame. Fix before any render.
3. MOTION PASS. Camera moves only where `reason` exists; stillness where the camera array is empty; parallax from `depth`; label and counter arrival from `enterAtWord`. Use the style tokens' easing; never linear. A scene that holds still for longer than about 1.2 s must be a declared end-of-scene hold or have a moving visual, otherwise `make_allow.py --check` warns and the gates will fail it.
4. TRANSITION PASS (complex ones last). Operators: `dissolve`, `match_geometry`, `focus_bridge`, `truthful_morph`, `match_movement`, `nested_scale`, `depth_through_object`, `occlusion_reveal`; everything else is a cut. Anchored operators need `anchorFrom` and `anchorTo` (validation fails without them) and a rationale; locked ones clamp the size ratio they can bridge (`match_geometry` and `truthful_morph` 8×, `focus_bridge` 2×), and `make_plan.py` warns when your anchors ask for more, in which case pick a simpler operator or resize the anchors. Write a transition card `qa/transitions/<sceneId>.md` for each (before, after, what the viewer tracks, anchors, easing, duration, risks, fallback). Do not overuse them: a transition is justified by the story, and a cut or a hold is first-class.
5. DRAFT RUN through the real gates (small and fast; production size comes in stage 05):
   `python3 tools/pipeline.py run --project videos/<slug> --size 640x360 --auto-downgrade`
   It renders the long video and every validated Short from the same `scenes.json`, then runs media QA and motion QA. If motion QA fails a complex transition, `--auto-downgrade` replaces it with a dissolve, records why in `render/downgrades.json` and re-runs the same gates (at most 2 rounds). Look at what the machines cannot judge: `render/previews/<target>/tr-<from>-<to>-strip.png` and the short clips next to it (every transition window with its audio), `qa/sheets/<target>/` (contact sheets), and the hold and silence spans the plan declared. Write what you saw, briefly, in `qa/visual-review.md`. Fix, at most 2 cycles per item. Exit 1 = a gate FAILED (read its message: it names the time range and the cause), 3 = BLOCKED (a tool could not run or be trusted), 5 = the creative preview is not approved.
6. CAPTIONS come from the alignment (`out/captions.srt`, and word-by-word captions inside the portrait band for Shorts); the pipeline writes them.
7. Update `library/lessons.md` with anything a future video should know (label each line observed or hypothesis).

EXIT CHECKS: the draft run reaches every target · media QA and motion QA are PASSED, or every FAILED/BLOCKED item has a recorded cause and fix · every non-trivial transition passed its gate or was downgraded and logged · `qa/visual-review.md` written.
