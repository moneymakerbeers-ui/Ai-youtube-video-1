STAGE 03 - VOICE AND SOUND. Follow CLAUDE.md §5 and `reference/voice-direction.md`. Video folder: `videos/<slug>/`.
RESUME: existing takes are kept; a finished or submitted paid take is never requested again (the ledger knows). Spend only inside the ceiling the creator approved for THAT service (`python3 tools/ledger.py --project videos/<slug> status`).

1. CHOOSE the voice by topic fit and audience, not by habit; fix voice, model and base settings for the whole video; write the reason in `audio/voice.md`. Stacks (CLAUDE.md §5): `elevenlabs` (needs a key and the creator's approval), `file` (takes made elsewhere: Higgsfield voice through the MCP tools, a human, any studio), `flite` (free local TTS, robotic, always available when the doctor shows libflite). Without an approved paid stack the video is made with `flite` and the report says so; that is a quality limit, never a block.
2. PLAN THE TAKES. `python3 tools/voice.py --project videos/<slug> --plan-only` writes `audio/take-plan.json`: each take (scenes grouped into a paragraph-scale arc), the exact text the synthesiser receives (only `scenes[].voice.pronunciation` respellings are applied; `spoken_script.txt` and `scenes[].narration` are never modified, and captions use the plain narration), its character count, and each validated Short's own opening/closing line. The tool does not compile bracketed audio tags: do not add any unless you produce the takes yourself on a model that documents them, and then follow `reference/voice-direction.md`.
3. PRODUCE THE TAKES. VOICE ID AND MODEL LIVE IN THE PROJECT, NOT IN A FLAG YOU MIGHT FORGET: write `config.json` → `"voice": {"elevenlabs": {"voiceId": "<ID>", "model": "eleven_multilingual_v2", "baseSettings": {"stability": 0.55}}}` once (stage 03 step 1); `voice.py`, `pipeline.py --stack auto` and `/make` read it, a CLI flag overrides it, and a missing id is BLOCKED (exit 3) with the fix, never a crash. Emotion is EXECUTED, not just recorded: each take's `emotion/intensity/pace/emphasisWords` become real synthesis instructions for the chosen model family (`tools/voice_direction.py`, shown per take in `audio/take-plan.json` → `synthesis`): v2-class models (multilingual_v2, turbo, flash) steer by `voice_settings` (stability, style, speed) and receive plain text; v3-class models (`eleven_v3*`) receive an audio tag (`[warmly]`, `[with wonder]`, …) and stability ∈ {0.0, 0.5, 1.0}. The narrator stays the same voice: only bounded deltas around `baseSettings` are applied. After the takes, `audio/voice-continuity.json` compares loudness and pitch per take with the median and WARNS (heuristic, for a human to listen) when one take drifts. Audition the emotional extremes before the full run: `--sample "<passage>" --sample-emotion wonder --sample-intensity 3`. STATUS: the request shape is tested against a local mock; how the real ElevenLabs voices sound with it is UNTESTED until you listen to the audition.
   - `flite`: `python3 tools/voice.py --project videos/<slug> --stack flite [--voice slt]`.
   - `elevenlabs` (approved only): `python3 tools/voice.py --project videos/<slug> --stack elevenlabs --voice-id <ID> [--model eleven_multilingual_v2]`. Exit 3 = BLOCKED (no key), 4 = refused (not approved, over the ceiling or the quota), 5/6 = a resume found an earlier request that must be looked up first (see `/make resume`). A crash and re-run never pays twice: finished takes are reused, submitted ones are fetched from the provider's history. Live ElevenLabs is UNTESTED here (only a local mock server was used): check the first take by ear and by `ledger.py status` before generating the rest.
   - Higgsfield voice (approved only): per take in `audio/take-plan.json` wrap the MCP `generate_audio` call in `ledger.py intent` (price first, key `higgsfield:take-<id>`) → `submitted` (as soon as there is a job id) → `done --file audio/takes/<takeId>.wav`; then `voice.py --stack file`. If the sandbox cannot download the result, mark it BLOCKED and fall back to `flite`; never fake a take.
   - `file`: put `audio/takes/<takeId>.wav` in the project and run `voice.py --stack file` to validate and register them.
   - Audition before the full run when a paid stack is used: `python3 tools/voice.py --project videos/<slug> --sample "<the hardest passage>" --sample-out audio/audition.wav` (free with `flite`; spend-gated, ledgered and reused with `elevenlabs`). Judge emotion by ear; no proxy replaces that.
4. ASSEMBLE AND ALIGN (the pipeline does both; this is how to run only the audio): `python3 tools/pipeline.py run --project videos/<slug> --only audio`. `assemble` places the takes with true silence for the declared pauses (never crossfading through words); `align` aligns the assembled audio against the script and writes `audio/alignment.json` with its source, coverage and quality numbers (engines: `dtw` against a same-voice reference, TESTED only for `flite`; `elevenlabs` forced alignment, UNTESTED live; `whisper` is not implemented and is BLOCKED; with a non-flite voice and no ElevenLabs key `auto` falls back to `dtw` against a flite reference and the result is marked APPROXIMATE: sentence and scene boundaries hold, word boundaries can be off by several hundred ms). Alignment that is not trustworthy (low coverage, non-monotonic, outside the file) is BLOCKED: animation timing cannot be trusted, so say so instead of continuing.
5. EACH SHORT'S OWN AUDIO. A Short is not a slice of the long video's timeline: `short_audio.py` (a pipeline step) assembles its scene windows plus its own opening/closing lines in the same voice and aligns *that* audio again, so a Short with new voice lines has its own aligned timing. Check `audio/shorts/<id>/` has `narration.wav` and its alignment, and that every Short is ≤ 180 s and within about 25 % of its target length; trim or re-scope now, not in stage 05.
6. WRITE TIMING BACK into `scenes[].timing` from the alignment (contiguous scene windows, each boundary at the midpoint of the silence between scenes; contract in `reference/voice-direction.md` §6), then `npx tsx schema/validate.ts project videos/<slug>`. A production long video under 5:00, or any Short over its limit → return to stage 01 step 7 for that part, once.
7. MUSIC AND SFX are optional and licensed: `audio/cue-sheet.json` per scene (music none/start/continue/duck/stop, SFX events with the word they land on and why, planned silences). A music bed is used only if `inputs/music/<file>.license.json` sits next to it (`mix.py --music`); it is looped, faded and ducked under the voice by sidechain. "None" is a valid answer; silence under a hard explanation or serious material is normal. Log every licence in `credits.md`.
8. LEVELS. The final mix is two-pass loudnorm (measured, then linear gain) to the engineering target in CLAUDE.md §5, done by the pipeline's `mix` step; the voice keeps its dynamics.

EXIT CHECKS: takes registered (`audio/takes.json`) · `alignment.json` trustworthy (or BLOCKED with the cause) · `scenes.json` timing written and valid · every validated Short has its own audio and alignment · ledger within each service's ceiling · the voice stack and any FALLBACK recorded in `audio/voice.md`.
